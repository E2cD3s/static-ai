Talk to Static

Static is an AI regular in this server's voice channels and chats. Talk to it out loud like anyone else in the call, or message it. It has opinions, looks things up, sets reminders and clips funny moments, and it all runs on its owner's own PC. It's on Discord and on our own Fluxer server too - same personality, a few differences.

Using
  1. Join a voice channel and type /join.
  2. Start with its name: Static, what should I play tonight?
  3. Just talk. It answers everyone in the channel.
  4. Keep talking. Talk over it to cut it off.

Talking in voice

Outside a conversation it only answers when it hears its name, anywhere in the sentence: Static, what do you think? works, and so does what do you think, Static? Once it has answered you, you have about 20 seconds to reply without the name. That window adjusts itself to how your server talks.

It answers everything said in the channel, so there's no need to use its name.

It sticks with the person it's talking to. Others chatting nearby won't set it off. Anyone can join in by saying its name, and then they're part of the conversation too.

It also answers to , in case the speech-to-text mishears its name.

Taking turns

It waits for you to finish, and gives you a moment longer if you trail off mid-thought. When several people talk at once, it waits until everyone's done and answers them together. To cut it off, just talk over it: it turns itself down straight away, and stops once you've been talking for about a second. A laugh or a quick "yeah" won't stop it. Only the person it's answering (or already talking with) can stop it that way; anyone else talking just turns it down. /stop silences it instantly.

Cut it off by accident? Say go on, keep going or what were you saying? within a minute and a half and it picks up where it stopped, without starting over.

What it knows

It knows who's in the call and recognises people by their account, not their nickname. It can see what you're up to on Discord, like the game you're playing or your music, and brings it up now and then. For news, scores, prices, releases or anything recent, it searches the web first and answers from what it finds. Over time it keeps short notes on people, so it remembers what you're into. It also remembers the group's big moments and inside jokes, and brings them back up when they fit. Maths, unit conversions and "what time is it in Tokyo?" are worked out exactly, not guessed.

You can ask about anyone in the server, even if they're not in the call: Static, what roles does Casey have? or Static, list my roles. It answers from the server itself, not the web, and says so if it can't find someone.

Looking out for people

If nobody's talking to it but someone sounds genuinely heated or down for a while, it may chime in once to check on them, sincerely or with a joke, taking into account what they're doing (a rough match or a bad pull). It keeps this rare: at most once every 10 minutes, and it never lectures.

Sending it away

Say Static, you can leave now or useUse /leave. It also leaves on its own when everyone else has gone. Asking works. Force-disconnecting it doesn't go unanswered, though: it comes straight back to say a few words to whoever kicked it, then leaves on its own terms. After a long quiet spell it forgets the conversation, so the next call starts fresh.

Text chat

Same personality, in writing. It doesn't answer every message, only when you call it:

    When you call it in, it reads the last 10 messages first, so it knows what you're talking about.

    It can see images. Post one (or reply to one) and ask about it. It can also look at profile pictures: Static, what do you think of my pfp?

    @mention people in your message and it knows who you mean. Questions about members and roles work here too.

    Its messages never ping anyone, even if someone tries to make it.

    Things to ask for

    Ask out loud or in text, in your own words. These all work:

    Reminders
    Static, remind me in 20 minutes to take the pizza out Static, remind everyone at 9 we're raiding Static, cancel the pizza reminder

    Your own reminders arrive as a DM. Group ones ping everyone in the channel. If you're in voice when a reminder is due, it says it out loud too.

    Polls
    Static, make a poll: pizza or tacos

    It creates a real Discord poll. It posts the choices with number reactions (1️⃣ 2️⃣ …): vote by clicking one. It closes the poll on time, posts the result and announces the winner in voice. Or type it: !poll Pizza tonight? | yes, no | 10 minutes

    Roasts
    Static, roast jordan Static, talk shit about him Static, roast me

    It goes after whoever you name (or "him"/"her": the last person who spoke), using what it knows about them, what they're playing and what they just said. All in good fun.

    Clips
    Clip that! Static, clip the last 10 seconds

    It saves the last 30 seconds of the call (up to a minute) as an MP3 in the voice channel's chat. If two people ask at once, you get one clip, not two.

    Quote book
    Static, quote that Quote what riley said Static, give us a random quote

    Someone said something legendary? "Quote that" saves the last line someone else said (or "quote me" for your own) to the server's quote book and posts it in the chat. Pull one up any time with /quote, or search it: /quote search:mob masher. To save a text message, right-click it, then Apps, then Save as quote. Pull one up any time with !quote, or search it: !quote mob masher. To save a text message, reply to it with !quote.

    Game night
    Static, flip a coin Static, roll 2d6 Static, pick someone Static, split us into two teams Static, pick between tacos, pizza or sushi

    Coins, dice (d20, 2d6+3), a random number, picking a person or an option, and splitting everyone in the call into teams. The randomness is real (done by the computer, not made up by the AI), so nobody can accuse it of cheating.

    Weather
    Static, what's the weather in Denver tomorrow? Static, is it gonna rain in Chicago today?

    Live forecasts for anywhere, today, tonight, tomorrow or later this week. Leave out the place and it uses the local weather. Say which city.

    Discord and Fluxer

    Static is on Discord and on our own Fluxer server (), with the same personality, voice and features. Use the Discord / Fluxer switch at the top of this page to see the commands for the one you're on.

    • On Fluxer, commands start with ! instead of /, private answers come by DM, and polls are reaction polls.
    • It keeps a separate memory on each: its notes on people, server memories, quotes and reminders on Fluxer aren't mixed with Discord's, unless you link your accounts.

    Linking your accounts (optional). Use /link on Discord to get a code, then type !link 123456 on Fluxer within 10 minutes (or start with !link on Fluxer and enter the code with /link). After that it knows both accounts are you and shares what it remembers about you between them - nothing else. /unlink or !unlink undoes it, and so does /forget.

    Privacy and memory

    It keeps

      It doesn't keep

        /profile shows what it has written down about you, /forget deletes it, and /profiling stops it taking notes on you. Server admins can wipe a channel's current conversation with /reset.

        The owner can read text logs of what it heard and said, for fixing problems.

        How it works

        Every spoken reply goes through four stages, all on one home PC with one graphics card.

          It feels quick because it starts early. During your pauses it already transcribes what you said and starts on a reply, then plays it only once you've actually finished. It speaks the first sentence while the rest is still being written.

          It also learns the room. If people keep cutting it off, it keeps its replies shorter. It picks up on mood from your words and tone of voice, and learns which way of responding goes over best with your crowd.

          Live speed and response times are on the status page.

          Troubleshooting

          It isn't answering me in voice

          Check it's in your voice channel (/join from where you are). Say its name: it only answers outside a conversation when called. If it's talking with someone else, say its name to jump in. Also check you're not muted and your mic works for other people.

          It stopped talking, or didn't stop, when someone spoke

          It only stops once someone has been talking over it for about a second, so laughs and quick reactions don't cut it off. Only the person it's answering can stop it; anyone else just turns it down, so a side conversation won't interrupt it. If it stopped when it shouldn't have, say go on.

          It misheard me

          Names, game terms and slang trip up speech-to-text the most. Say it again more clearly, or type the tricky word. Names it has just said are easier for it to catch.

          I can't DM it, or it can't DM me

          This is usually your Discord privacy settings. Open the server menu, go to Privacy Settings and turn on Direct Messages.Check Fluxer's privacy settings allow direct messages from server members. Also check you haven't blocked it. If a reminder can't reach you by DM, it pings you in the channel instead.

          "Clip that" doesn't post anything

          It posts clips in the voice channel's chat, so it needs permission to send messages and attach files there. Temporary channels often don't give bots that, so ask a server admin. It tells you out loud when Discord refuses.

          Fluxer voice channels have no chat, so clips go to the text channel where you typed !join (or #general). It needs permission to send messages and attach files there.

          It said something wrong

          It's a small AI model on a home PC and it can get facts wrong, especially from memory. For anything current, ask it to look it up: it then answers only from what it finds.

          A reply took longer than usual

          That's usually a web search (you'll hear "let me look that up"), or several people talking at once so it waited for everyone to finish.
